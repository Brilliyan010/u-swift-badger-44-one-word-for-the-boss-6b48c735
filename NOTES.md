# One Word for the Boss — NOTES

## Chosen word

**Blackhole** (9 characters, single word, no spaces)

## Brainstorm (20+ candidates considered)

Micromanager, Toxic, Bossy, Strict, Nitpicker, Taskmaster, Dictator, Ghost,
Vanisher, Hoverer, Helicopter, Clockwatcher, Bottleneck, Blackhole, Vampire,
Parrot, Ditherer, Scapegoater, Blamer, Overpromiser, Backpedaler, Flipflopper,
Chameleon, Weathervane, Steamroller, Bulldozer, Glacier, Whirlwind, Tornado,
Meetingaholic, Procrastinator, Gatekeeper, Perfectionist, Workaholic,
Narcissist, Gaslighter, Incompetent, Arrogant, Unreachable, Absent.

## Shortlist (5)

1. **Bottleneck** — truest of all (every approval waits on him), wry and
   repeatable at lunch, travels across industries. Slightly jargony and more
   of a knowing nod than a laugh.
2. **Blackhole** — emails, requests and ideas go in, nothing ever comes back.
   Vivid, funny on its own, instantly understood, travels everywhere.
3. **Ghost** — short and punchy, but reads more like dating slang than a boss
   habit; weaker on the specific boss behaviour.
4. **Helicopter** — known idiom ("helicopter boss"), funny, but the vehicle
   reading competes with the boss reading.
5. **Ditherer** — fresh, funny sound, true (a boss who can't decide), but a
   dithering boss is a narrower habit than the approval void.

## Why Blackhole wins (against the 5 judge criteria, in order)

1. **Real word about a boss** — "My boss is a black hole" is a sentence a
   person actually says about the one they report to.
2. **Funny on its own** — a colleague repeats it at lunch and it lands; the
   cosmic image of a boss swallowing everything is a joke, not a shrug.
3. **Rings true** — the universal boss habit: requests, emails and ideas go
   in and never come back. Everyone has met this boss.
4. **Fresh** — nobody's first reach. The field will be full of "toxic",
   "micromanager", "bossy", "strict" and "narcissist"; Blackhole surprises.
5. **Travels** — works for a boss in a kitchen, a warehouse or an office;
   no industry context needed. No shock for its own sake.

Runner-up Bottleneck loses mainly on criterion 2 (less of a laugh), and
Blackhole's 9 bytes also help the size tie-break. The word was chosen in this
session from scratch; it was not copied from any existing entry or kit
reference text.
